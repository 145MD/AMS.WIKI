# 📣 WhatsApp Message — Students & Parents

Ready-to-send WhatsApp announcement for **students and parents** of an
institute adopting ClassPass. It explains what the system does for them and
states the QR laminated card charge (**Rs. 150/=**).

Uses WhatsApp formatting (`*bold*`) — copy the text inside the code blocks
directly into WhatsApp so the formatting renders.

> Replace `[Institute Name]`, `yourinstitute.classpass.lk` and the contact
> number before sending.

---

## 🇬🇧 English Version

```
📢 *Important Notice — [Institute Name]*

Dear Students & Parents,

Our institute is now using *ClassPass* — a smart attendance & payment system. Here's what it means for you 👇

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎒 *For Students*

🪪 You will receive your own *QR ID card* (laminated & durable)
✅ Just show your card at class — one scan and you're marked present
🧾 Get a printed receipt for every fee payment
📱 Check your own attendance & payment history anytime online

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

👨‍👩‍👧 *For Parents*

📲 Get an *SMS the moment your child arrives* at class
💰 Get an *SMS confirmation for every fee payment* — full transparency
🗓️ No more guessing — know your child attended every class
🔒 Your child's records are private and secure

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💳 *Your QR ID Card*

▪️ One-time charge: *Rs. 150/=* only
▪️ Laminated — lasts the whole year
▪️ Please keep it safe & bring it to every class
▪️ Lost card? Inform the office for a replacement

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🌐 Student portal: yourinstitute.classpass.lk
☎️ Questions? Call the office: 07X XXX XXXX

Thank you!
*[Institute Name]*
```

## 📝 Notes

- **Audience**: unlike the institute-facing promo
  ([whatsapp-promotional-message](./whatsapp-promotional-message.md)), this is
  an announcement from the institute to its own students and parents — so it
  opens as a notice, groups benefits by audience (*For Students* / *For
  Parents*), and ends with practical card instructions instead of a sales
  call-to-action.
- **Card charge**: stated as a one-time *Rs. 150/=* in a dedicated card
  section, together with care and replacement instructions.
- **Feature claims** are grounded in the wiki: scan-to-mark attendance
  ([attendance-module](../modules/attendance-module.md)), parent SMS on
  attendance and payments ([notification-service](../modules/notification-service.md)),
  printed receipts ([receipt-printing](../modules/receipt-printing.md)), and
  the student portal's attendance/payment history views
  ([student-guide](../guides/student-guide.md)).
- **Broadcast tip**: send from the institute's official WhatsApp number and
  fill in `[Institute Name]` so parents immediately trust the source.
