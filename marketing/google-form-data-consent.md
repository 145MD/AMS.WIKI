# 📋 Google Form — Student Data Collection Consent Term

Consent/privacy term for the Google Form institutes use to collect student
data before onboarding to ClassPass. States that the information will be used
for the ClassPass system and related institute communications/promotions, and
will not be shared with external parties.

**Usage in Google Forms:**

1. Paste the notice into the form **description** (or a Section header) so
   respondents read it before filling anything in.
2. Add a required **checkbox question** at the end using the short consent
   line, so submission itself records agreement.

> Replace `[Institute Name]` and the contact number before publishing the form.

---

## 🇬🇧 English Version

**Form description / section text:**

```
📋 Data Collection & Privacy Notice

The information you provide in this form (student and parent/guardian details) will be collected by [Institute Name] and used only for:

• Registering the student in the ClassPass attendance & fee management system — including issuing the QR ID card and sending SMS notifications to parents
• Institute announcements and promotional communications related to classes and services

Your information will be stored securely within the ClassPass platform and will NOT be shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any external parties.

You may contact the institute at any time to view, update, or request removal of your information.

☎️ Questions: 07X XXX XXXX
```

**Required checkbox question:**

```
I have read the Data Collection & Privacy Notice above and consent to my information being used as described.
☐ I agree
```

## 📝 Notes

- **Scope of "not shared"**: the notice says data is stored *within the
  ClassPass platform* rather than claiming it never leaves the institute —
  ClassPass (and its SMS gateway) process the data to deliver the service, so
  a blanket "never shared with anyone" would be inaccurate. The promise made
  is the one that matters to parents: no disclosure to external parties for
  their own use.
- **Consent is recorded**: the required checkbox turns each form submission
  into a recorded consent, which is stronger than a notice alone.
- **Keep promises consistent**: the promotional-use clause covers the
  institute's own announcements and promotions (matching the WhatsApp
  messages in this folder). If data would ever be used beyond that, the
  notice must be updated *before* collecting under the new purpose.
